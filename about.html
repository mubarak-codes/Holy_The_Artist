ABOUT ME

I'm Mubarak Owosheni Oluwatunmishe — Holy The Artist.

I make art using letters, numbers and symbols.

My path into art wasn't planned. I came across a TikTok comment where someone had drawn a house using nothing but simple text characters and emoji. I'd never seen anything like it. I started researching how it was done, and realized barely anyone was actually doing this. So I tried it myself.

That was the beginning. Some pieces are built on my phone, others are hand-drawn on paper, turning plain characters into a portrait, character by character.
