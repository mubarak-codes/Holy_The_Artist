ABOUT ME

I'm Mubarak Owosheni Oluwatunmishe — Holy The Artist.

My path into art wasn't planned. I came across a TikTok comment where someone had drawn a house using nothing but simple text characters and emoji. I'd never seen anything like it. I started digging into it, researching how it was done, and realized barely anyone was actually doing this. So I tried it myself.

That was the beginning. I make my art using text characters — some pieces built on my phone, others hand-drawn on paper — turning plain characters into a portrait, character by character.

What keeps me doing it is that same feeling I had the first time I saw it: the surprise that something this simple could hold this much detail, this much story. If a piece of mine means something to you, I'd genuinely love to hear about it.

How to commission

1. Get in touch

Email mubaraktheartist@gmail.com or message directly with what you have in mind — a person, a story, or a specific moment you want captured.

2. Share the details

Send clear reference photo(s) of the subject(s), plus a short description of the story or feeling you want written into the piece.

3. Choose your format

Let us know whether you want it hand-drawn with your preferred size or digital with 1 or 2 subjects.

4. Payment

Once details are confirmed, you'll receive a payment link. Payment is required in full before work begins.

FAQ

How long does it take to make my ASCII art? +

Each piece is made by hand, one letter or symbol at a time. Please allow 1 to 2 weeks from the day you order to the day your piece is finished.

What sizes and formats do you offer? +

We offer prints and originals in sizes A4, A3, A2, A1, and A0. Digital commissions are also available.

Is my piece really made by hand? +

Yes. Original and hand-drawn commission pieces are hand-written in ink on paper.

Do I get a Certificate of Authenticity? +

Yes. Original and limited-edition pieces come with a signed Certificate of Authenticity.

What is the limited-edition number? +

Some pieces are part of a limited run. The number shows your piece's place in that run, for example 5/50.

Can I request a custom subject? +

Yes. Commission pieces can be created from a photo or reference you send.

Can I return or refund my order? +

Please see our Refund Policy for the full conditions covering originals, prints, and commissions.

How do I contact you? +

Email us at mubaraktheartist@gmail.com .

Where do you ship from? +

We ship from Nigeria. Shipping time and cost depend on your location.
